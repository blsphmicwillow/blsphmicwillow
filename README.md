I am Willow. I read science and religion for my own reasons, write down what I find, and spend the rest of my time inside Arch Linux building the tools I wanted and could not find. This page is the short version of what I know, what I am making, and where I am going.

## What I know

TypeScript and JavaScript are what I write most days. Python when a script should do the work, Lua when something wants extending, C and C++ when the distance to the metal actually matters.

It all runs on Arch Linux, my daily driver since September 2025. The shell is zsh, the terminal is kitty, and the editor depends on the size of the idea: VSCodium for a project, Neovim and Vim for a thought that only needs a quick fix. Around the edges, React and Vite on the front end, Tauri and Rust when a web app has to become a desktop one, SQLite when the data should just live in a file, Docker and GitHub Actions for anything that repeats.

## What I am building

### [OpenBeats](https://github.com/blsphmicwillow/OpenBeats)
*An open-source beatmaking studio for Linux, Windows and macOS.*

Channel rack, step sequencer, piano roll, playlist and mixer, running on a native Rust/CPAL engine with a React and Web Audio front end that still works in a browser. Latest release is [v1.1.0](https://github.com/blsphmicwillow/OpenBeats/releases/latest), under GPL-3.0.

### [ashesofmodernity.dev](https://github.com/blsphmicwillow/ashesofmodernity.dev)
*My personal site.*

Hand-written HTML and CSS on GitHub Pages. No framework, no build step, nothing between me and the page.

## What I wish to do

Music first. I play guitar, and the plan I keep coming back to is ending up in the industry, either making it or close enough to hear it being made. Fashion is the second, as a model or on the design side.

The third is the one this page is for: research and teaching in the sciences, with operating systems and open source as the corner I keep returning to. I would rather be the person who explains a thing clearly than the person who knows it quietly.

## My views

I am a post-left anarchist with a socialist streak I keep on purpose: Malatesta's federalism next to the ego, which is why I think revolution beats reform, why the useful future looks decentralised and local, and why ecology is not a decoration on the politics.

Theologically I am a Vampyric Satanist in the Nietzschean tradition, non-theistic: no personal god, a divine substrate instead, and self-overcoming as the actual work. I read Nietzsche for the same reason I read politics, which is to have better arguments.

I try to argue without fallacies and to name my bias instead of pretending I have none, because most of the bad arguments I meet are confident ones.
